// Module 01 — The Basics

What is ransomware?

Ransomware is harmful software that can lock your files or computer. Criminals then demand money to restore access. Paying does not guarantee that your files will be returned.

🔒

Files become locked

Photos, documents, and important work files may become impossible to open.

💬

A payment is demanded

A message may demand money to unlock your files, sometimes with a countdown.

🛡️

Prevention matters

Careful clicking, software updates, and reliable backups help reduce the risk.

How can an attack happen?

01

A message arrives

A fake delivery notice or unexpected email catches your attention.

02

A risky action

Someone opens a harmful attachment or runs an unsafe download.

03

Files get locked

Ransomware may scramble files so they cannot be opened.

04

A ransom note appears

Criminals demand payment, but recovery is not guaranteed.

What if you suspect ransomware?

Stop interacting with suspicious content. If this is a work device, contact your IT or security team immediately and follow their instructions. Don't pay a ransom or try unfamiliar fixes on your own.
